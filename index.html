As a degreed Mechanical Engineer, I don't deal in pipe dreams—I deal in real-world, highly accurate fluid systems. With a strong foundation in advanced CAD, fluid dynamics, and heavy mechanical systems, I deliver engineering solutions that prioritize precision, strict compliance, and operational reliability.
